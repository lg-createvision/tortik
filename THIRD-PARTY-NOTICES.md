UI research: official 21st.dev community examples for media expansion, image focus and editorial gallery interactions. The three page compositions are custom React/CSS implementations. No external UI service is required at runtime.

Magic UI BlurFade is adapted locally in src/components/BlurFade.tsx with prefers-reduced-motion support. Dialog and Button primitives are vendored under src/components/ui.
